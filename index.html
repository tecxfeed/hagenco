At Hagenco, we specialize in OEM and bulk manufacturing of T-shirts, jerseys, kidswear, womenswear, and custom apparel for brands, events, schools, corporates, and institutions.

Based in Tiruppur — India’s textile hub — we combine modern machinery with skilled craftsmanship to deliver high-quality, durable, and stylish garments at scale.
