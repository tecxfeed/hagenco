About us

About FISMO HAGENCO

FISMO HAGENCO manufactures and exports apparel for brands, retailers, corporates, events, schools and institutions. We make T-shirts, polos, shirts, innerwear, hoodies, jerseys, kidswear and uniforms, from small brand launches to large bulk orders.

Our production is based in Tiruppur, India's textile and knitwear hub, where modern machinery meets skilled craftsmanship. Our head office is in Kozhikode, Kerala. As a trusted garment manufacturer and exporter from India, we supply brands and wholesalers across India, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Afghanistan, the USA, Canada, the UK, Germany, France, Spain, Italy, the Netherlands and Australia.
